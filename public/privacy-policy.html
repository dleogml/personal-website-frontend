Privacy policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

This policy explains how Unbox Korea handles information when you visit this website, including the cookies and similar technologies used by advertising and other service providers.

Information processed when you visit

Our hosting and delivery provider, Cloudflare, may process technical information needed to deliver and secure the site, such as your IP address, browser and device details, requested page, and request time. See Cloudflare’s privacy policy.

The sign-in and account form shown on the home page is currently a visual prototype. Submitting it does not create an account or send or store the email address or password through this website.

Google advertising, cookies, and similar technologies

Unbox Korea uses Google AdSense advertising technology. Google and other advertising partners may use cookies or similar identifiers to serve and measure ads. These technologies may use information about your visits to this site and other sites, including your IP address, browser or device information, and interactions with ads. Depending on your settings and applicable requirements, this may include personalized advertising.

Learn how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can manage Google’s personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings or opt out of some participating companies’ personalized advertising at aboutads.info. You can also manage or delete cookies through your browser settings.

Third-party content and services

The home page loads fonts from Google Fonts and a background photograph from Unsplash. When your browser requests these resources, those providers may receive technical connection information such as your IP address and browser details. Their services are governed by their own policies: Google Privacy Policy and Unsplash Privacy Policy.

This site may link to other websites. We do not control their privacy practices, so please review their policies when you visit them.

How information is used and retained

Technical information is used by our service providers to deliver, maintain, and protect the site. Advertising providers may use information for ad delivery, frequency controls, fraud prevention, and measurement, subject to their policies and your settings. Unbox Korea does not currently operate an account database or analytics service. Service providers retain information under their own policies and legal obligations.

Your choices and applicable rights

You can adjust cookie controls in your browser and manage personalized advertising through the links above. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights concerning personal information. Google’s consent requirements may also apply to visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland; where required, consent must be collected through an eligible consent flow before personalized advertising is served. See Google’s consent management guidance.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site and its services change. The latest version will be published on this page with its updated date.

This policy describes the site’s current setup. If we add account features, analytics, or additional advertising partners, we will update this page to explain those changes.
